English

Fixes:
- Trust and Untrust finish at once, without waiting for a rescan (it took about 40 seconds before).
- Turning the block on or off no longer waits for a scan that is running.
- After Remove completely, the list refreshes in the background.

Limits:
- Not phone-tested yet.

How to test:
1. Install as a module update through the root manager, then reboot.
2. Open the 📱 Apps tab, press Rescan, then right away press 🛡️ Trust on an app: it must finish within a few seconds.
