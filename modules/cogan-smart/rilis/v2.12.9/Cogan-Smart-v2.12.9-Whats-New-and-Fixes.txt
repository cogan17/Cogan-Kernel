English

Fixes:
- Screen-off time in the temperature chart is now drawn blue instead of an empty gap. With the screen off the phone sleeps and takes no sample; that gap is now a blue line. A gap from a restart stays open.
- Includes v2.12.8 (no cap ping-pong) and v2.12.7 (social media cap).

Limits:
- Not phone-tested yet.

How to test:
1. Install as a module update, then reboot.
2. Turn the screen off for a few minutes, turn it on, open Temperature → Summary 1 hour: the screen-off time must be a blue line.
