Cogan Smart Kernel v2.12.6 / versionCode 37 — paket uji / test build

Indonesia

Perbaikan:
- "Terapkan ulang tiap interval" yang dulu tersimpan diam-diam oleh tombol Paksa (v2.12.4 ke bawah) dilepas sekali saat update. Mode, target suhu dan setelan cas tetap.
- Riwayat Daya tidak lagi menulis "Badan HP turun" saat yang dingin hanya prosesornya. Kini: "Badan HP … °C, di bawah batas …" atau "Prosesor … °C, di bawah 55 °C: batas dilepas".
- Termasuk semua perbaikan v2.12.5 (hemat baterai saat layar mati).

Batas:
- Belum diuji di HP.

Cara uji:
1. Pasang sebagai pembaruan modul melalui root manager, lalu reboot.
2. Tab Daya (Mode Dev): centang "Terapkan ulang tiap interval" harus mati.
3. Setelah HP sempat dibatasi lalu dilepas, riwayat Daya menulis salah satu teks baru di atas.

English

Fixes:
- "Reapply at each interval", saved behind the scenes by the Force button (v2.12.4 and older), is switched off once on update. Mode, temperature target and charging settings stay.
- The Power history no longer writes "Phone body down" when only the processor cooled. Now: "Phone body … °C, under the … limit" or "Processor … °C, under 55 °C: cap released".
- Includes every v2.12.5 fix (less battery use with the screen off).

Limits:
- Not phone-tested yet.

How to test:
1. Install as a module update through the root manager, then reboot.
2. Power tab (Dev mode): "Reapply at each interval" must be off.
3. After the phone was capped and released, the Power history shows one of the new texts above.
