BAHASA INDONESIA

1. PENAMBAHAN

- Mode Hemat, Kernel Default dan Performa kini membaca suhu Badan HP
  (sensor kulit HP, yang terasa di tangan), bukan prosesor terpanas.
  Ambang: Hemat 39 °C, Kernel Default 41 °C, Performa 44 °C.
- Langkah halus: mulai 1 °C sebelum ambang dengan dua langkah ringan.
  Di ambang, CPU besar turun 10% per langkah sampai 60%, lalu CPU kecil
  ke 90% dan 80%. Tiap langkah ditunggu tiga kali cek; CPU naik lagi
  setelah tiga kali cek minimal 1,5 °C di bawah ambang.
- CPU hanya diturunkan saat prosesor bekerja (60 °C atau lebih). Panas
  dari cas, ruangan atau HP yang santai tidak membuat CPU diturunkan.
- Rem darurat di semua mode: CPU turun setiap cek bila prosesor 95 °C
  atau lebih.
- Penyebab panas per bagian HP, masing-masing dengan saran: cas cepat,
  cas, grafis (GPU), modem (data seluler), prosesor, proses latar
  belakang, proses kernel, layar mati, serta layar & pemakaian biasa.
  Tiap momen panas dijelaskan dari sekitar 3 menit terakhir.
- Kartu Rem HyperOS di tab Daya (baca saja): suhu papan, profil termal,
  batas CPU, modem dan Wi-Fi, kecerahan maks serta mode video yang
  dipasang HyperOS sendiri.
- Suhu papan HyperOS (board_sensor_temp) ikut dicatat tiap sampel sebagai
  sensor hyperos_board di grup Badan HP.
- Node GPU HP ini (devfreq Mali, GED, gpufreqv2) tampil di Kontrol
  kernel. Baca saja; tidak ada yang ditulis.

2. PEMBARUAN

- Tab Suhu: "panas" berarti Badan HP 41 °C atau lebih. Grafik, penyebab
  panas, rata-rata/tertinggi per aplikasi, kernel dan mode, serta
  notifikasi panas memakai Badan HP. Prosesor tetap tampil.
- Tab Daya menampilkan Badan HP sebagai suhu utama, prosesor di
  sebelahnya, dan penanda rem darurat.
- Tanpa sensor Badan HP, suhu baterai yang dipakai.
- Teks WebUI Indonesia dan English, serta versi cache WebUI v2.9.0.

3. PERBAIKAN

- Di v2.8.2, pengendali membandingkan prosesor terpanas (sekitar 50 °C
  saat santai) dengan ambang 42 °C. Menurut log 3 hari owner, ambang itu
  terlewati 97% waktu, sehingga CPU besar ditahan 40% hampir sepanjang
  waktu. Sekarang ambang dibaca dari Badan HP.
- Ambang suhu lama (v2.8 dan build uji v2.9.0 sebelumnya) diganti dengan
  bawaan baru. Mode, setelan cas dan interval tetap seperti disimpan.

4. FITUR DAN PENGATURAN YANG DIPERTAHANKAN

- Jeda cas karena suhu oleh modul tetap nonaktif secara bawaan:
  HyperCharge dan sistem HP yang mengatur suhu cas.
- Auto cut charging berdasarkan persentase tetap bekerja: bawaan berhenti
  di 95% dan melanjutkan di 90%. Batas yang disimpan pengguna tetap dipakai.
- Modul tidak menonaktifkan driver termal, tidak menulis node HyperOS
  atau GPU, dan tidak mengubah negosiasi charger, arus maupun tegangan.

5. PENGUJIAN DAN BATAS VALIDASI

- Lulus tes pengendali dan pencatat dengan sysfs tiruan, memakai mksh
  dan One True Awk, serta dash dan mawk.
- Simulasi pada log owner (layar menyala, ambang 41 °C): CPU penuh
  sekitar 69% waktu pada data 3 jam dengan HyperCharge, 85% pada data
  1 jam dan 92% pada log 3 hari. CPU besar tidak pernah di bawah 70%.
  Angka ini dihitung dari log, belum diukur di HP.
- Kartu Rem HyperOS dan saran penyebab diperiksa di browser Chromium
  dengan HP tiruan, bahasa Indonesia dan English.
- Uji HP owner pada versi sebelum ambang 41 °C: Badan HP terbaca dari
  quiet_therm, tab Daya/Suhu/Modul bekerja, saklar cas input_suspend
  ditemukan. Versi ini sendiri ditulis sebelum uji HP.

6. CARA MEMPERBARUI

1) Pasang Cogan-Smart-Kernel-v2.9.0.zip sebagai pembaruan modul melalui
   root manager, lalu reboot perangkat.
2) Tutup dan buka ulang WebUI Cogan Smart.
3) Ambang suhu baru dipakai otomatis; reset tidak diperlukan.


ENGLISH

1. ADDED

- Power saver, Kernel Default and Performance now read the phone body
  temperature (the skin sensor, what a hand feels) instead of the hottest
  processor sensor. Thresholds: Power saver 39 °C, Kernel Default 41 °C,
  Performance 44 °C.
- Smooth steps: from 1 °C before the threshold only two light steps. At
  the threshold the big cores drop 10% per step down to 60%, then the
  little cores to 90% and 80%. Each step waits three checks; the CPU
  steps back up after three checks at least 1.5 °C below the threshold.
- The CPU is only lowered while the processor works (60 °C or more).
  Heat from charging, the room or an idle phone does not lower the CPU.
- Emergency brake in every mode: the CPU steps down at every check when
  the processor reaches 95 °C or more.
- Heat causes per part of the phone, each with a tip: fast charging,
  charging, graphics (GPU), the modem (cellular data), the processor,
  a background process, kernel processes, screen off, and the screen &
  ordinary use. Each hot moment is explained from the last ~3 minutes.
- HyperOS brakes card in the Power tab (read only): board temperature,
  thermal profile, CPU, modem and Wi-Fi limits, maximum brightness and
  video mode as set by HyperOS itself.
- The HyperOS board temperature (board_sensor_temp) is logged with every
  sample as the sensor hyperos_board in the phone body group.
- This phone's GPU nodes (Mali devfreq, GED, gpufreqv2) show under Kernel
  controls. Read only; nothing is written.

2. UPDATED

- Temperature tab: "hot" means a phone body of 41 °C or more. The chart,
  heat causes, per-app, per-kernel and per-mode average/peak, and the
  heat notification use the phone body. The processor still shows.
- The Power tab shows the phone body as the main temperature, the
  processor beside it, and an emergency-brake marker.
- Without a phone body sensor, the battery temperature is used.
- WebUI texts in Indonesian and English, WebUI cache version v2.9.0.

3. FIXED

- v2.8.2 compared the hottest processor sensor (about 50 °C at rest)
  with a 42 °C threshold. In the owner's 3-day log that threshold was
  exceeded 97% of the time, so the big cores were held at 40% almost all
  the time. The thresholds now read the phone body.
- Earlier temperature thresholds (v2.8 and the earlier v2.9.0 test build)
  are replaced by the new defaults. Mode, charging settings and interval
  are kept as saved.

4. RETAINED FEATURES AND SETTINGS

- Module temperature-based charging pauses stay disabled by default:
  HyperCharge and the phone manage charging temperature.
- Percentage-based automatic charge cutoff remains available: defaults
  stop at 95% and resume at 90%. Previously saved limits remain in use.
- The module does not disable thermal drivers, does not write HyperOS or
  GPU nodes, and does not change charger negotiation, current or voltage.

5. TESTING AND VALIDATION LIMITS

- Controller and recorder tests passed on a simulated sysfs with mksh and
  One True Awk, and with dash and mawk.
- Simulated on the owner's logs (screen on, 41 °C threshold): full CPU
  about 69% of the time in the 3-hour data with HyperCharge, 85% in the
  1-hour data and 92% in the 3-day log. The big cores never went below
  70%. Computed from the logs, not measured on the phone.
- The HyperOS brakes card and the cause tips were checked in a Chromium
  browser with a simulated phone, in Indonesian and English.
- Owner's phone test of the version before the 41 °C thresholds: the
  phone body reads from quiet_therm, the Power/Temperature/Modules tabs
  work, charging switch input_suspend found. These notes were written
  before the phone test of this version.

6. HOW TO UPDATE

1) Install Cogan-Smart-Kernel-v2.9.0.zip as a module update through your
   root manager, then reboot the device.
2) Close and reopen the Cogan Smart WebUI.
3) The new temperature thresholds apply automatically; no reset is
   required.
